Emotion: Controlled enthusiasm. Let big moments register, but never lose composure. The excitement is in the timing, not the volume.

Emphasis: Hit final scores, player full names on first mention, and decisive action words — "walk-off," "complete game," "sixth save." Land the numbers cleanly.
